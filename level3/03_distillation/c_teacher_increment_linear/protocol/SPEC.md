Hypothesis: learning the teacher's increment over an out-of-fold baseline avoids mixing teacher out-of-fold targets with overconfident in-sample B1 offsets. A compact, regularized sequence-context regressor may learn additional sequence signals more effectively than the short-trained random trimer network.
