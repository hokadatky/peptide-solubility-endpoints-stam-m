# Research-assistance and literature provenance

Experimental-design skill used to specify comparison arms, split nesting, pre-fit gates and the separation between exploratory development and confirmation. Research assistance, not empirical evidence: Kassis T, Agarwal V, He Y, Patel D, Brueckner AM (2026). Scientific Agent Skills: A Library of Procedural Knowledge for Research Agents. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.00065 (current record verified 2026-09-09).

Conceptual prior: Li X, Li S, Bourahla O, Wu F, Li X. ResKD: Residual-Guided Knowledge Distillation. IEEE Transactions on Image Processing (2021), https://doi.org/10.1109/TIP.2021.3066051; primary preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04719. The paper explores residual-guided teacher/student compression in image classification. It motivates studying residual information, but does not establish protein-solubility performance or validate this cross-fitted probability-increment implementation. No novelty of residual distillation in general is claimed.

Implementation reference: https://xgboost.readthedocs.io/en/stable/parameter.html, accessed 2026-09-09; actual local XGBoost version3.1.3 is recorded in preflight, not inferred from the newer online version. Only locally available dependencies and STAM-M input data are used.
